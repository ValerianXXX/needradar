# Portfolio Claim Register

This register keeps interview and resume language aligned with the evidence.

## Supported claims

- Designed and implemented an evidence-first AI opportunity-intelligence product.
- Built a governed pipeline that stores, screens, structures, clusters, drafts, compares, and publishes public signals.
- Implemented source-policy, privacy, evidence-completeness, and duplicate-suppression controls.
- Delivered a public product with English as the default and a persistent Chinese-language switch.
- Produced a production snapshot containing 421 stored observations, 117 public observations, and 10 published opportunity briefs on 26 August 2026.
- Defined a human/AI operating model and a validation ladder from public signal to sustained outcome.
- Built automated quality checks and documented the boundary between system output and market validation.
- Shipped a read-only opportunity handoff and project registry that connect independent portfolio projects without shared runtime coupling or circular demand claims.

## Claims that require qualification

- “Validated demand” is valid only for an opportunity that reaches the corresponding evidence level.
- “Automated publication” describes policy execution, not proof that every published opportunity deserves investment.
- “Multilingual” describes product presentation and source handling, not perfect cross-language semantic equivalence.
- “Connected portfolio” describes a documented, read-only handoff contract; it does not mean the projects share a runtime, automatically exchange private data, or validate one another.

## Unsupported claims

- Customer adoption or active-customer counts.
- Measured reduction in research time or workflow cost.
- Revenue, savings, ROI, or willingness to pay.
- Product-market fit.
- Enterprise deployment or organizational change outcomes.
- Superior duplicate-detection accuracy without a labeled evaluation set.

## Resume-safe wording

> Designed and implemented NeedRadar, an evidence-first AI opportunity-intelligence product that converts governed public signals into traceable, deduplicated opportunity briefs. Defined source, privacy, similarity, publication, and human-decision controls; shipped an English-first bilingual product and documented the boundary between production output and market validation.

## Interview follow-up

If asked for impact, lead with the implemented decision system and production counts. Then state that the next impact test is a bounded real-workflow pilot measuring preparation time, reviewer agreement, task outcomes, and adoption behavior.
