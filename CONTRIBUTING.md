# Contributing

NeedRadar welcomes corrections to the public methodology, policy examples, and documentation.

Before opening a change:

1. Keep every repository artifact in English.
2. Do not submit copied private content, credentials, or personal data.
3. Distinguish a public observation from validated demand.
4. Preserve counterevidence, unknowns, and source-policy boundaries.
5. Run `npm run check`.

An opportunity proposal should describe a real task, the current workaround, the desired outcome, why AI may fit, the human decision gates, and at least one reason the idea may not deserve investment.
